AI Booking Platform

Case study

Booking engine + AI assistant

A booking engine any appointment business can run from configuration, with an AI assistant that answers from the business's own knowledge base and never acts without the user's confirmation. Each business is defined by a single profile: its names, booking rules, and knowledge.

Try it: one click as a client, advisor, or admin; no sign-up.

  • 93%cached input, lower cost
  • 700+automated tests
  • 120live AI checks passing
  • 2businesses, one codebase

Engineering highlights

  • The AI can't act alone. Suggested bookings and cancellations need your confirmation before they run.
  • A knowledge base admins run themselves. Add, edit, or upload documents, group them in sections, and pick which roles may read each; the assistant cites only what that user can see.
  • Answers follow the settings. Change a booking rule in Admin and the assistant quotes the new value, with citations.
  • Secure by default. Roles are re-checked on every request, every input is validated, and sign-in and the assistant are rate-limited.

Student-Faculty Services v2

Case study

Capstone rewrite in plain PHP and React

A full rewrite of my senior capstone: students book 5 to 45 minute office-hour slots, and faculty set their hours, closures, booking rules, and approvals. Undo, reminders, ratings, and a waitlist are built in.

Try it: one click as a student or faculty member; no sign-up.

  • 0double bookings in an 8-student race
  • 46API endpoints, no framework
  • 100/100Lighthouse accessibility

Engineering highlights

  • Ten booking rules, one transaction. Notice, overlaps, limits and closures are checked together, all or nothing.
  • Undo that re-checks every rule. Cancellations and closures can be undone for 10 seconds, and the slot stays held until then.
  • Reliable reminders. One a day before, in the app or by email, cancelled with the appointment and never sent twice.
  • Tested before every deploy. 500+ PHPUnit, Vitest, and Playwright tests, including accessibility checks, must pass before GitHub Actions deploys.
